NEW CAR BUYERS STUDY (NCBS)

Representative annual study among new car buyers (max. 6 months after purchase, registrations from 9/2023 - 8/2024) collecting data on
satisfaction with recently purchased car, sales service, brand loyalty, market structure, and consumer characteristics.

Brand KPIs Definition:

Brand loyalty rate:
Share of previous brand owners who bought a new car model of the same brand again

Brand consideration rate:
Share of brands of all main alternative car
