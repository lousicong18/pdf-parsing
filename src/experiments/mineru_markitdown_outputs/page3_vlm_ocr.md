**NEW CAR BUYERS STUDY (NCBS)**

Representative annual study among new car buyers (max. 6 months after purchase, registrations from 9/2023 - 8/2024) collecting data on satisfaction with recently purchased car, sales service, brand loyalty, market structure, and consumer characteristics.

---

**Brand KPIs Definition:**

**Brand loyalty rate:**
Share of previous brand owners who bought a new car model of the same brand again

$$VW \ brand \ loyalty \ rate = \frac{New \ car \ buyers \ who \ purchased \ VW \ and \ owned \ VW \ as \ previous \ car}{Respondents \ who \ owned \ VW \ as \ previous \ car} \ \%$$

**Brand consideration rate:**
Share of brands of all main alternative car

**Brand conquest rate:**
Share of conquered customers of all new customers the brand potentially could have won (replacers coming from other brands, additional car buyers, first time buyers)

$$VW \ brand \ conquest \ rate = \frac{New \ car \ buyers \ who \ purchased \ VW - New \ car \ buyers \ who \ purchased \ VW \ and \ owned \ VW \ as \ previous \ car}{Total \ new \ car \ buyers \ market - Respondents \ who \ owned \ VW \ as \ previous \ car} \ \%$$

**Migration balance:**
Balance (sale units) of won customers (gained from other brands) and lost customers (previous brand owners not buying the brand again)
