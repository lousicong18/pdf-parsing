## 关键问题点（带红旗标识 🔴）：

**🔴 Overall seating comfort（整体座椅舒适性）：**
- Insufficient amount of support at the lower-back area（下腰部支撑不足）
- The rear seats sitting position is too upright（后排座椅坐姿过于直立）

**🔴 Easy to use inf. & entertainment system inc. in-car apps（易于使用的信息娱乐系统及车载应用）：**
- Since processing chips are outdated, which often leads to system randomly crashing, long responsive time（处理芯片过时，常导致系统随机崩溃、响应时间过长）
- Voice control often fails to understand commands（语音控制常无法识别指令）

**🔴 Features of inf. & entertainment system inc. in-car apps（信息娱乐系统及车载应用功能）：**
- Limited number of APPs are supported by the system（系统支持的应用数量有限）

**🔴 Integrated navigation system（集成导航系统）：**
- In-car navigation has bad signal（车载导航信号差）
- Taking too long to calculate route and arrival time（路线及到达时间计算耗时过长）
- Lacking regular map updates（缺乏定期地图更新）
